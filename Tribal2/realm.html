How to Play (click to expand)

The loop: resources generate automatically → spend them on Buildings, Barracks, and Stable → use the map to Attack, Support, Scout, or gift resources to allies → queue things up and come back later.

Buildings

  • Headquarters — speeds up all other building upgrades
  • Timber Camp / Clay Pit / Iron Mine — boost that resource's production rate
  • Warehouse — raises how much of each resource you can store (numbers turn red when you're nearly full and wasting production)
  • Farm — raises your population cap (how many troops you can have)
  • Barracks — speeds up training of Spearmen, Swordsmen, and Militia
  • Stable — speeds up Scout training
  • Wall — boosts your defense when raided

Troops

  • Spearman — cheap, balanced, decent at everything
  • Swordsman — expensive, strongest attack, best for raiding
  • Militia — cheap, fast, strong defense, but can't carry loot — a pure home guard
  • Scout — no attack, no defense, no carry. Purely for reconnaissance — see "Scouting" below

Raiding & Support

  • Attacking sends troops on a real trip — travel time depends on distance
  • Losses are proportional on both sides — a near-even fight hurts everyone, not just the loser
  • Support stations troops at another village to help defend it — recall any time, or Recall All if you're supporting several
  • Set an alliance tag in the Account panel — same-tag villages show up blue on the map and get a friendly-fire warning before you attack them
  • Same-tag villages can also send each other resources directly from the raid panel
  • Every raid produces a full Battle Report — click one to expand the breakdown of troops, losses, and loot

Scouting

  • Send scouts to a village from its raid panel — if you send more than they currently have stationed at home, their exact resources and every building's level get revealed to you
  • The reveal goes to your own event log only — it's never shown anywhere public, and the target is never told they were scouted
  • Scouts never fight and always return home safely, whether the mission succeeds or fails

The world

  • The realm is a 20×20 world — your map shows an 8×8 window into it, use the arrows or Recenter to look around
  • The World Overview mini-map below it shows every village at a glance — click a dot to jump there
  • Nearby Villages lists your closest neighbors; Watchlist lets you pin anyone regardless of distance — both jump straight to their raid panel

Staying informed

  • Realm Activity shows raids and support happening across the whole world, not just your own
  • The header shows how many lords are online right now
  • An incoming attack (not support, not a gift) flashes the browser tab and plays a sound once — mute it in the header if you'd rather not hear it
